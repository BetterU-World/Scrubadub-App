# Subscription compliance review — November 11, 2026

Historical initial investigation. **The implementation and current release requirements are now documented in `subscription-release-readiness.md`, which supersedes the unimplemented-work/blocker lists below.**

Initial status: **incomplete draft; release blocked**. No deployment, email, promotion creation, or live billing changes were performed. This is an engineering assessment, not a legal compliance opinion.

## Repository findings

- `convex/actions/billing.ts` (signed-in owner) and `publicBilling.ts` (public signup) create Stripe subscription Checkout sessions, each with `trial_period_days: 14` and `allow_promotion_codes: true`. No application-defined six-month coupon or discount calculation exists. Prices come from `STRIPE_PRICE_SCRUB_{SOLO,TEAM,PRO}`. Public checkout uses an attempt UUID for Stripe idempotency.
- Checkout previously had no explicit consent collection. This draft uses Stripe's required unchecked terms checkbox, with recurring-billing text, rather than adding a second application checkbox. Stripe enforces submission. The selected price is retrieved from Stripe; unsupported pricing fails closed instead of displaying misleading monthly terms.
- A signed platform `checkout.session.completed` event records acceptance, exact custom text, terms version, customer/subscription/session IDs and event timestamps. Repeated events cannot add records. Legacy sessions remain supported without fabricated acceptance. No public consent read API was added. Retention/deletion policy must be approved before production (including California's applicable retention requirements).
- `convex/http.ts` verifies webhook signatures and separates platform events from Connect events. Subscription created/updated/deleted events sync status, price and period end. Existing event claims prevent duplicate completed-event processing.
- `convex/mutations/billing.ts` stores `currentPeriodEnd`, status and cancellation flag; the current data model has no reliable first-positive-charge date or promotional entitlement end. Trial end and discount end are not stored. A billing period end is not necessarily the first paid charge.
- `subscriptionGating.ts` / `stripeSubscriptionCompatibility.ts` allow active/trialing access; cancel-at-period-end does not immediately revoke it. Once Stripe marks canceled, there is no separate six-month entitlement. Cancellation during the initial trial or a free discounted month may end access before the advertised six months. Do not claim that existing behavior guarantees the offer.
- Settings > Billing > Manage Billing calls the authenticated owner portal action. Whether the portal allows online cancellation and when it takes effect are Stripe Dashboard configuration, not proven by this repository.
- Existing Resend transport lives in `convex/lib/email.ts`; notification actions reuse it. No subscription-specific durable confirmation was found. Stripe's configured subscription email/receipt settings are not in source control. Adding a second sender without inspecting those settings risks duplicate confirmations.
- Convex cron currently runs calendar sync, upload cleanup and operational-email recovery. The reliable operational-email outbox is explicitly tied to jobs and assignment/completion/approval events. It is not a generic subscription notification queue. No subscription reminder scheduler was found.

## Legal sources reviewed November 11, 2026

- [ROSCA, 15 USC 8403](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap110-sec8403.htm): online negative-option sales require material disclosures before billing information, informed consent and a simple mechanism to stop recurring charges. No universal federal seven-day reminder is inferred.
- [California Attorney General alert](https://oag.ca.gov/node/608083): applicable free/discount periods exceeding 31 days require a notice 3–21 days before expiration; qualifying initial terms of at least one year require notice 15–45 days before renewal. A seven-day extended-promotion reminder fits the former window, not the latter. [BPC 17602 official statute](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=17602.&lawCode=BPC) could not be fetched (403); counsel must confirm current text, including annual continuous-service reminders and consent retention. Monthly billing does not establish an exemption from annual continuous-service duties.
- [Colorado official 2025 Title 6, section 6-1-732](https://olls.info/crs/crs2025-title-06.pdf), pp. 101–103: durable trial acknowledgment and cancellation before payment; renewal notices 25–40 days before applicable renewals. For terms under 12 months, the notice tracks renewals extending service past successive continuous 12-month periods. This is not a seven-day trial reminder rule. [Enacted SB25-145](https://www.leg.colorado.gov/bills/sb25-145) also strengthens online cancellation. Confirm later amendments and B2B/consumer applicability with counsel before release.
- [FTC's March 2026 rulemaking notice](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/p064202negativeoptionruleanprm.pdf) describes the 2024 amended rule's vacatur. Do not present that vacated rule as currently binding; ROSCA and applicable state laws still require review.
- [Stripe coupon documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/coupons): repeating coupons run from application, not necessarily after trial; a six-month discount is not proof of six additional calendar months of entitlement. [Checkout API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/create) documents required terms collection and custom text.

4. Reminder implementation must reconcile authoritative subscription/invoice/discount data, separately represent promotion expiry and first paid charge, recheck immediately before delivery, suppress canceled/nonrenewing accounts, and use transactional deduplication plus auditable retry outcomes. It must cover existing accounts, webhook-before-public-setup races and changed billing dates. These checks remain unimplemented, not passing tests.
