# Founder notifications V1

Server-only operational email to one mailbox. No frontend API, inbox, tenant-visible recipient, or analytics changes.

## Exact triggers

- **New Company Signup:** `authInternal.provisionPublicCheckout`, after the atomic company/site/owner/Stripe linkage and `checkoutProvisioning` insert; also `authInternal.createUser` when legacy `authActions.signUp` creates an active owner. Key: `signup:<companyId>`. Creating a company without an owner, incomplete checkout, worker invitations, clients, affiliates, frontend showcase data, and QA direct inserts do not produce signup mail.
- **Subscription/Trial Started:** active/trialing evidence from the verified platform `customer.subscription.created/updated` webhook, or the subscription retrieved by `completePublicSetup`. Evidence is retained before company creation and reconciled at provisioning. Key: `access:<companyId>:<subscriptionId>`. A real active owner and matching Stripe company linkage are required. Repeated updates and trial-to-active transitions do not resend for that subscription. Resubscription with a different subscription ID is a distinct access milestone. Includes actual status, price/known tier, trial end and discount/coupon/promotion IDs when supplied by Stripe; a discount does not imply a trial or a payment. Older unexpanded discounts can only supply their discount IDs.
- **First Paid Subscription:** signature-verified, platform-account `invoice.paid` records an invoice-keyed verification job. Cron schedules read-only Stripe verification separately from webhook/canonical affiliate processing. Retrieve the invoice, validate customer/subscription linkage, inspect paid subscription invoice history, and inspect paid InvoicePayment allocations. Require a succeeded PaymentIntent with positive `amount_received`, or succeeded captured standalone Charge, matching customer/currency and positive invoice allocation. Zero invoices, credit-only settlement, manual/out-of-band paid invoices, and payment records without collection proof do not qualify. A partial discount with actual positive collection qualifies. Key: `paid:<companyId>` across all subscriptions. Modern `parent.subscription_details.subscription` and legacy subscription IDs use the existing compatibility helper.
- **Assessment Completed:** `assessments.complete` after required-answer validation, scoring, giveaway validation, completion snapshot and milestone persistence. Key: `assessment:<attemptId>`. Repeated completion, in-progress/abandoned attempts do not resend. Only score/result, completion timestamp, ID and contact already available at completion are included. Anonymous assessments usually have no contact; contact collected later for report delivery is not retroactively emailed. Existing `/admin/assessments/:attemptId` route is included with configured APP_URL at delivery; ordinary admin authorization remains required. No capability/report token or answers are included.
- **Giveaway Entry:** shared `recordGiveawayEntry` after the actual qualified entry insert, for assessment and alternate methods. Key: `giveaway:<entryId>`. Includes method, qualified/confirmed eligibility, consent, campaign, timestamp and assessment reference where applicable. Uses existing campaign enablement, approved rules and time window without changes. Assessment completion alone is not an entry. Duplicate entries across methods do not resend.

## Delivery and failure semantics

Canonical mutations atomically append small durable outbox rows. A one-minute Convex cron schedules committed work in bounded batches (25 emails, 10 verification jobs). No email provider request or delivery scheduling occurs inside signup/assessment/giveaway transactions. All new registered functions are internal; lookups use indexes. Existing operational email delivery and recipient behavior remain unchanged.

Email reuses the Resend client, platform sender headers, HTML escaping/detail rows and conservative provider outcome classifier in `lib/email.ts`. Each message has branded responsive-width HTML, text fallback, a distinct subject and a stable Resend key `founder/<deliveryId>`.

Email claims are atomic and single-use. V1 makes at most **one provider attempt per event**, including definite rejection; automatic scheduler/action repeats cannot resend. Missing/invalid recipient is `skipped` with a configuration warning. Definite rejection is `failed`; timeout, malformed reply, provider uncertainty, or an expired 10-minute claim is `unknown`. Acceptance persists its provider message ID. If Resend accepts but acknowledgement persistence fails, the claim becomes `unknown`, and is never retried automatically. This deliberately favors avoiding duplicate mail over guaranteed delivery. Review failed/unknown/skipped rows server-side; do not reset unknown claims without independently checking provider acceptance. No new general retry platform or operator UI is introduced.

Stripe verification retries only read operations: four attempts, 15 minutes apart. A crash leaves the due job eligible for its next bounded attempt. Exhaustion leaves `failed`; warning messages contain no provider error body, email addresses or secrets. Invalid linkage or unverified payment never creates conversion mail. History is paginated with a 1,000-invoice safety bound; exceeding it requires manual investigation rather than guessing. Once a company's conversion row exists, renewals skip history reads. History lookup chooses the earliest verified collection even when webhook order is reversed. On rollout, the next invoice event for an existing company can produce a **single historical first-payment notification** with the original invoice/time and `historical_payment` context; it never mislabels the renewal itself as the first invoice. There is no proactive historical scan/backfill.

## Configuration and release

Required server-side Convex variable: `SCRUB_FOUNDER_EMAIL`, a single bare mailbox. Reuse existing `RESEND_API_KEY`, verified `RESEND_FROM_EMAIL`, `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, platform webhook signing secret, and `APP_URL`. Existing external-side-effect kill switch/local URL protections remain effective. No `VITE_` variable or frontend configuration is needed. No configuration was changed as part of this PR.

After founder review/approval, release the additive schema (three small indexed tables), internal actions/mutations and cron with the normal Convex release. Configure the mailbox before enabling delivery to avoid skipped events. No migration/backfill is required. This PR does not merge or deploy, and does not invoke `convex dev`, `convex deploy`, or live email/Stripe calls for validation.

## Manual smoke checklist after an approved release

- Use a test deployment, Stripe test mode and a controlled founder mailbox; verify the existing sender/domain configuration.
- Complete public checkout and setup: one signup and one access message; retry setup. Try an incomplete checkout and worker/client/affiliate flows: no signup mail.
- Exercise a real trial and an active 100% promotion: access statuses correct; no paid mail for zero/credit-only invoices.
- Collect a positive subscription payment with a partial coupon: one first-payment message with verified original amount/currency/invoice. Replay signed events, reverse subscription/payment event order, and run a renewal: no duplicates.
- Complete a non-giveaway assessment twice: one assessment message, no giveaway message. Confirm the admin link requires admin authorization.
- During an actually open campaign, submit assessment and alternate entries for the same email: one entry message across methods. Test closed/ineligible submissions: no entry mail and existing behavior preserved.
- Remove the founder recipient and simulate Resend rejection/timeout in test mode: canonical records remain committed, delivery states are skipped/failed/unknown, and replay does not send twice.
- Inspect provider acceptance followed by a failed acknowledgement write: claim becomes unknown after 10 minutes; no automatic resend.

## Automated validation

Focused tests cover provisioning, legacy owner vs invited worker/affiliate creation, trial/promo vs payment, positive discounted cash vs zero/credit-only collection, signed webhook and invoice deduplication, out-of-order history, renewal suppression, bounded verification retries, assessment completion vs incomplete/repeated completion, giveaway lifecycle/deduplication, and single-use delivery claims and provider failures. Existing giveaway, Stripe compatibility, checkout hardening and operational email tests are included in the regression run. Full typechecks, frontend build and `git diff --check` are recorded in the PR.
