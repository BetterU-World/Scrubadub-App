## Consideration and legal-source notes (checked November 11, 2026)

The seven-minute assessment is not redesigned. A mandatory substantial-effort entry condition can raise consideration questions depending on jurisdiction; no blanket conclusion that assessment completion is prohibited or exempt is made. The already-existing equal-odds free alternate online method is retained, prominently disclosed and available to prior participants. Counsel should approve its sufficiency and advertising for the eligible states.

[Florida's official game-promotion statute, 849.094](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/849.094) prohibits requiring a fee/payment/purchase to enter and addresses awarding advertised prizes and certain filing/security requirements based on total announced value. [FDACS guidance](https://www.fdacs.gov/Business-Services/Game-Promotions-Sweepstakes/What-makes-a-game-promotion-unlawful) also identifies failure to award prizes and purchase-conditioned entry as unlawful. Applicability, current amendments and other states require legal review; being below a listed filing threshold is not nationwide clearance. [FTC sweepstakes guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/fake-prize-sweepstakes-and-lottery-scams) reinforces that receiving a real prize should not require payment. These sources do not decide the optional subscription offer's prize valuation or sponsor-address requirements.

## Fair, auditable drawing and fulfillment (after authorization)

1. Designate a restricted ledger/storage location and responsible operator. After closing, select **#2** in Admin > Giveaways, load every page until exhausted and export. Alternatively paginate `giveaways.entrants` under a verified superadmin session until `isDone` using the established #1 process. No assessment answers/scores are needed. Freeze export, timestamp, campaign ID, approved rules version, row count and SHA-256. Protect emails, session tokens and verification documents; never put them in a public PR.
2. Check campaign/time/rules scope and normalized-email uniqueness. Apply the one-person rule consistently, document duplicates/reasons, and retain original rows. Business eligibility can be provisionally attested then verified for the selected entrant. Do not exclude somebody merely for using alternate entry, having no assessment, previously taking an assessment, declining marketing, or declining the optional offer. Existing account holders at entry and RI residents are ineligible under #2's approved rules; use reasonable evidence and record the decision rather than an opaque automatic heuristic.
3. Freeze/hash an ordered array of eligible unique Entry IDs (one position each; do not use assessment scores, marketing, subscription preference or method as weights). Use a uniform draw, e.g. Node's `crypto.randomInt(pool.length)`. Record the array hash, count, random index, selected ID, operator, timestamp and rules version in the private append-only ledger before notification. Preserve the result; do not reroll silently. This is the same manual process as #1.
4. Verify the selected entrant. After founder authorization to send notification, record exact notification time, channel, message reference and response deadline **notification + 72 elapsed hours**. Keep correspondence privately. Selection alone sends nothing and creates no Stripe code.
5. Record response and verification outcome. If ineligible, gift-card-declining, unreachable or unresponsive by the deadline, append the reason and evidence; preserve the first draw. Exclude prior selected/disqualified entries, freeze/hash the remaining eligible pool, perform and record an equally weighted alternate draw linked to its predecessor. Use the same criteria and response window. Declining only the SCRUB offer never triggers replacement.
6. Fulfill the verified winner's **$50 Visa gift card** independently via the established digital email delivery process. Record provider/transaction reference and fulfillment time privately. Do not request a subscription, card, purchase or marketing opt-in. Target December 25; escalate any verification delay rather than misrepresenting delivery. Optional offer acceptance/decline, private code delivery and redemption are separate ledger events after billing validation/authorization. Gift-card delivery does not wait for that choice.
7. Publish winner identifying information only with appropriate explicit permission. Retain original entries, frozen rules, export, draw/alternate events, correspondence and fulfillment evidence in the approved restricted location.

Suggested append-only ledger columns: event ID, campaign, approved rules version, timestamp, operator, event type, related event ID, pool file/hash/count, random index, selected Entry ID, notification reference/time, response deadline/time, verification result/reason, gift-card fulfillment reference/time, optional-offer choice/redemption reference. No live tracking or notification occurs from this document.
