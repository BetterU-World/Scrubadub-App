# Subscription implementation and release verification

November 11, 2026 follow-up to PR #290. This document supersedes the initial report's unimplemented-work list. No production deployment, customer email, live Stripe mutation or live promotion creation was performed.

## Implemented behavior

**Checkout and consent:** retains the original Stripe-price-derived disclosures, required hosted checkbox and signature-verified, versioned/deduplicated acceptance record. The general Terms effective date remains March 1, 2026; only the subscription section has a November 11 disclosure version. No duplicate application consent checkbox was introduced.

**Confirmation ownership:** SCRUB owns subscription confirmations and the reminders below when both `SCRUB_SUBSCRIPTION_EMAIL_OWNER=scrub` and `SCRUB_ENABLE_SUBSCRIPTION_NOTICES=true` are configured. Both are required; unset/off sends nothing. A newly accepted Checkout produces one durable plain-text confirmation through the existing platform Resend transport. It includes plan, actual regular price/frequency, Stripe trial end, applied discount details/expiry, verified winner award end/expected regular billing when known, online cancellation and management instructions. It does not guess a first-positive-charge date from a generic discount end or billing period. Credits/taxes may change the actual invoice total.

The signed platform Checkout event can enqueue confirmation before public account setup, but only when the accepted session's customer/subscription evidence matches. Existing accounts bind through their Stripe customer and subscription; replaced/mismatched subscriptions fail closed. Existing historical subscribers are not sent a second initial confirmation during background scanning. Connect events remain outside this path.

**Scheduling and delivery:** new billing-specific records reuse Convex crons/scheduling and Resend, without extending the job-specific operational outbox into unrelated billing events. Four-hour paginated reconciliation covers existing linked subscribers; signed subscription changes reconcile promptly. The five-minute delivery/recovery tick staggers sends. Each message has a unique subscription/kind/target key, frozen snapshot fingerprint, claim lease, attempt count, terminal outcome and optional provider ID. No recipient email, card details or raw provider errors are stored in the queue. Authorized operators can inspect these private tables in Convex.

- Standard trial notice: seven days before trial end.
- Extended promotion notice: seven days before the authoritative free/discounted period ends. For a verified winner, the entire free entitlement end is used; the initial 14-day boundary is not falsely announced as paid conversion.
- Annual continuous-service/renewal baseline: thirty days before the first actual renewal extending service past each continuous service anniversary. For monthly subscriptions this uses the billing anchor and calendar arithmetic, rather than assuming anniversary day equals renewal day. Annual-term subscriptions use their annual renewal, not initial trial conversion.
- Catch-up stays within the strict common window: trial/promotion messages stop three days before expiration; annual messages stop twenty-five days before renewal. Missed windows are recorded, not sent late. Canceled, nonrenewing, paused or inactive subscriptions receive no renewal reminders. Generic coupon expiration notices do not claim that expiration itself is the first charge.
- Each send re-fetches Stripe before claiming and again just before delivery; cancellation/plan/date changes suppress stale messages and reconcile current ones. Indexed transactional deduplication and stable Resend idempotency keys prevent concurrent duplicate requests. Rate limits/uncertain outcomes retry with the same content/key, at most five attempts inside twenty-three hours. Expired ambiguity becomes `unknown` for operator investigation, never an automatic retry after the provider's twenty-four-hour key lifetime. Late positive provider evidence can resolve uncertainty. Provider acceptance is recorded as acceptance, not proof of inbox delivery.

**Cancellation and awarded access:** Settings > Billing now has a direct authenticated owner-only Cancel automatic renewal button. It retrieves the company's actual Stripe subscription and checks its customer before updating anything. Ordinary subscriptions cancel at period end. A verified winner cancels at the **entire awarded end** via Stripe `cancel_at`, with no proration; monthly coupon billing boundaries cannot truncate the award. SCRUB continues using Stripe active/trialing status for access. There is no parallel entitlement engine. The separate cancellation timestamp is synced for the access-until display, without replacing the billing-period field used elsewhere.

During an active verified winner award, Manage Billing requires a dedicated portal configuration that disables portal cancellation and plan updates. Payment management remains there; the direct cancellation button remains available. This avoids an immediate or one-month portal cancellation bypass. After the award, ordinary portal behavior resumes. Dashboard/admin changes must preserve the recorded award; an early removed coupon, shortened trial or incompatible product restriction requires investigation rather than an invented free-end date.

## Coupon semantics: verified in deterministic tests, not against Stripe

A repeating six-month coupon starts when applied, which overlaps the trial. That fact alone does not establish the number of fully discounted invoices. The code verifies Stripe's returned 100% repeating-six-month coupon, actual start/end, standard fourteen-day trial, monthly fixed price and product applicability. All six monthly invoice starts after trial end must fall inside the discount window, and the following invoice must fall outside it. If that invariant holds, expected first regular-price billing is trial end plus six calendar months; the last free invoice can cover days after the coupon object's expiry.

The verified award date is recorded in Stripe metadata (`scrub_offer`, `scrub_winner_promotion`, `scrub_initial_trial_end`, `scrub_initial_discount_end`, `scrub_awarded_until`) so it survives normal coupon expiration. Metadata writes happen only for the configured winner promotion during explicitly enabled reconciliation. No existing historical subscriptions are rewritten by a migration. Deterministic tests cover all three plan prices, cancellation during trial and promotion, product/trial changes, coupon-window rejection and retained dates after normal expiration.

If the actual Test Clock matrix disproves the repeating-coupon invariant, `extendedWinnerTrial(startSeconds)` provides the smaller supported alternative: one Stripe trial ending fourteen days plus six UTC calendar months after the actual trial start, with the award date recorded in metadata and **no additional coupon attached**. It is a parameter builder, not a silently activated migration or public redemption flow. Exact date must be set from the actual subscription/trial start and disclosed in Checkout; do not compute it from an old invitation date. Verify this alternative in test mode before wiring the founder's offer to it. Never stack an extended trial and the six-month discount and accidentally award a different duration.

It must be a private winner-only, one-redemption 100% repeating-six-month offer valid for all offered plan products, with redemption expiry January 6, 2027 at 00:00 Eastern (January 5 11:59 PM included). Do not create a live promotion without separate authorization. Winner notification/verification and gift-card fulfillment remain independent of code redemption.
- Configure `STRIPE_WINNER_PORTAL_CONFIGURATION_ID` with subscription cancellation and subscription updates disabled during the awarded period. Verify the direct SCRUB cancellation button works before publishing the offer. Do not expose a default portal login/cancellation route that bypasses this restriction. Ordinary portal cancellation should be at period end, not immediate.
- Confirm one email owner. For this implementation, disable overlapping Stripe subscription-start confirmations/trial-ending reminders/renewal reminders and enable the two SCRUB flags above only after test verification. Keep payment receipts and payment-failure notices if desired; they are distinct transaction events, not substitutes for this durable subscription acknowledgment. Configure verified `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_FROM_EMAIL`, `APP_URL`, and Stripe's business Terms URL pointing to SCRUB's `/terms#subscriptions`. No Dashboard setting was changed by this task.
- Confirm the platform webhook endpoint subscribes to `checkout.session.completed` and subscription created/updated/deleted events. Existing signature verification, webhook deduplication and Connect separation remain mandatory.
- Before plan/price changes, use a future-dated process that sends any required material/fee-change notice. This PR's fresh-data reminder reconciliation is **not** an advance fee-change notification system. Disable immediate self-serve portal plan/price updates pending that process (winner portal already enforces this). Existing subscription prices are not changed here.
- On release, inspect `subscriptionNotices` for `missed`, `unknown` and persistent `failed` outcomes. Investigate provider IDs before any manual resend; do not clear deduplication records and replay old webhook events to force duplicate mail.

## Specific legal timing and records

Sources checked November 11, 2026: [California AG's current ARL guidance](https://www.oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-issues-consumer-alert-california%E2%80%99s-automatic-renewal-law) describes 3–21 days for qualifying extended free/discount periods, 15–45 days for qualifying annual-term renewals, annual continuous-service reminders, and 7–30-day advance fee-change notices. The chosen seven-day and thirty-day baselines are separate policies. [Colorado's 2026 official Title 6, §6-1-732](https://olls.info/crs/crs2026-title-06.pdf), pp. 169–172, retains the 25–40-day applicable renewal window, including renewals extending shorter contracts beyond successive continuous twelve-month periods, plus durable acknowledgment and accessible online cancellation. No Colorado universal seven-day trial-conversion mandate is inferred. [ROSCA](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap110-sec8403.htm) requires disclosures, informed consent and a simple cancellation mechanism; the previously vacated FTC rule is not treated as current authority. [Resend's idempotency documentation](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/idempotency-keys) establishes its twenty-four-hour retention window.

Consent records have no automatic deletion path. Restrict access and retain exact accepted text/version and Stripe event evidence for at least three years or one year after termination, whichever is longer where California applies; do not erase it in ordinary account cleanup without evaluating that obligation. Delivery records similarly need an approved retention/deletion policy; this PR does not invent a short purge window. Applying a practical baseline to every account avoids collecting state addresses solely to select notice timing. This is an engineering implementation of the identified requirements, not a nationwide legal certification; additional state or fee-change requirements must be assessed for the actual future change being made, not used as a generic stop condition.

External Stripe behavior and Dashboard configuration are explicitly unverified.
