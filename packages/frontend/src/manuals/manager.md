# Manager App Guide

Your Owner delegates specific responsibilities in SCRUB. This guide covers available Manager workflows; follow only those authorized for you. It is not a cleaning manual or permission to perform every Owner action. Some screens and permission labels remain in English even when Spanish is selected.

## 1. Your First Day and Delegated Responsibilities

1. Accept your Manager invitation, create your password, and sign in. Ask your Owner which responsibilities and permissions are currently enabled.
2. Open **Overview** to see the Operations Dashboard, then **Jobs** to review work you can access. Check dates, locations, status, and whether you oversee or actually execute each assignment.
3. Start with the next authorized task: inspect work, perform assigned cleaning, handle a request, or complete delegated administration. A page or job being visible does not grant every action on it.
4. Open **Manuals → Manager App Guide** for help. This guide requires no optional Manager capability. Use the [Cleaner App Guide](/manuals/cleaner) for detailed cleaning-form steps when assigned to execute cleaning.
5. Verify the saved record/status after acting. Ask the Owner about missing authority; do not borrow another person's account.

Owners can start from a preset and change any permission. Presets are not permanent access tiers:

- **Field Manager:** all-jobs visibility, work approval, rework, and red-flag resolution.
- **Scheduler:** all-jobs visibility, job creation/editing/cancellation, worker/team assignment, and schedules/Job Requests.
- **Office Manager:** clients/properties, sales/commercial operations, team operations, documents, invoices, and operational settings. It does not include schedule management or general financial reporting.
- **Operations Manager:** all 15 responsibilities listed below except Manage invoices and View financials.
- **Custom:** a different Owner-selected combination. Current enabled permissions, not the preset name, determine access.

## 2. Navigation and Missing Controls

Use **More** or the navigation menu on a phone. **Overview**, **Jobs**, **Red Flags**, **Notifications**, and **Manuals** provide starting points, subject to company access and record visibility. Other areas appear according to your permissions. Manager Company navigation may show **Documents**, **Worker Onboarding Documents**, **Add-Ons**, or **Inventory Templates**; do not assume access to the Owner Settings page.

These 15 responsibilities are independent. The Owner's permission editor uses the following labels; each can be enabled or disabled separately:

- **View all jobs and perform company-wide inspections** — broader visibility, not unrestricted editing.
- **Create, edit, and cancel jobs** — job administration, not automatic assignment authority.
- **Assign workers and teams to jobs** — supported assignment changes, not permission to create jobs alone.
- **Manage schedules and Job Requests** — scheduling, not all sales administration.
- **Request rework** — send submitted work back for correction.
- **Manage Quality Review — review and approve completed work** — operational approval, not compensation approval.
- **Resolve red flags** — supported issue-status changes.
- **Manage clients and properties** — client/location administration and supported portal access.
- **Manage sales and commercial operations** — leads, proposals, agreements, and commercial accounts.
- **Manage team operations** — supported team/invitation administration and Cleaner Fast Mode eligibility.
- **Manage documents** — supported resources, templates, and worker PDFs.
- **Manage invoices** — customer invoice operations, not all financial reporting.
- **View financials** — reporting, not authority to change outgoing payments.
- **View analytics** — operational reporting, not job or financial administration.
- **Manage Operational Settings** — supported add-ons/inventory configuration, not all company settings.

If a control is missing, check your current responsibility and the record's state with the Owner. Permissions can change during an active session. Some workflows need more than one permission; seeing a link is not proof that every next step is authorized.

## 3. My Jobs, All Jobs, and Assignment Types

**Requires for All Jobs: View all jobs and perform company-wide inspections.** Personal assigned-work access does not require that optional permission.

1. Open **Jobs**. With all-jobs permission, use **All Jobs** or **My Jobs**; without it, your results are limited to personal assignments and the toggle is absent.
2. Use date, status, and search filters. My Jobs includes work where you are the oversight Manager, an assigned executing worker, or an active member of the assigned team.
3. Open the job and check your role. An oversight assignment authorizes appropriate supervision; direct worker/team assignment is what matters for cleaning execution.
4. Verify current status before acting. All Jobs visibility does not itself grant editing, approval, invoicing, or execution.

A job you create without a personal assignment may not appear in your limited personal results. Ask the Owner to confirm responsibility. The job list is a viewing scope, not a promise that every separately delegated administrative action is limited to My Jobs.

## 4. Inspecting Work Versus Executing Cleaning

**Requires for execution: an active, compatible cleaning-executor assignment.** An oversight Manager assignment alone is insufficient.

1. Open a permitted job. Use **Open checklist** when offered to enter the shared work interface. If it is absent, check whether you are assigned to execute, rather than only supervise.
2. Accept/start work as the current job state requires. Complete checklist items individually, report required inventory, and confirm the actual performed workers during review.
3. Use **Review job → Submit job for review**, then verify **Submitted**. The form and job submit together; there is no second completion action. Final team submission also requires the appropriate lead/Manager authority.
4. For inspection rather than execution, use the inspection workflow in section 7. An inspection is not the same as starting or submitting cleaning work.

Managers cannot execute Maintenance jobs under the current role model and cannot personally use Cleaner Fast Mode. Execution still enforces assignment, inventory, worker evidence, and timer rules; a closed timer needs Owner attention. See the [Cleaner App Guide](/manuals/cleaner) for photos, flags, pauses, and rework mechanics.

## 5. Creating, Editing, and Assigning Jobs

**Requires: Create, edit, and cancel jobs. Assignment changes also require Assign workers and teams to jobs.**

1. Open **Jobs → Create Job**. Choose an active property, service type, date/time, duration, and instructions. Quick one-time creation is available through the creation screen's mode selector.
2. If authorized to assign, choose compatible active workers or an active team and the oversight Manager where appropriate. Team management and job assignment are different responsibilities.
3. Review the confirmation option, save, and verify the resulting job. A job can be created already Confirmed; that does not always mean a worker personally accepted it.
4. Use **Edit Job** for supported changes. Change assignments through that editing workflow when authorized; do not assume access to the Owner's separate Reassign control. To cancel, review the reason and verify Cancelled; Other requires an explanation.

Submitted/Approved jobs cannot normally be cancelled. Editing, cancellation, work review, and list visibility have separate authorization rules; do not treat My Jobs as a universal limit on delegated administration. If a created job disappears from your personal list, confirm its assignment with the Owner before recreating it.

## 6. Calendar and Existing-Client Job Requests

**Requires: Manage schedules and Job Requests.**

1. Open **Calendar**, choose month/week/day and filters, and open jobs to verify current details. Manager Calendar currently displays jobs; do not rely on it for walkthrough appointments.
2. Open **Job Requests** for an existing client's service request. Review client/location, requested service, preferred date/time, and pricing information. Requested preferences are not a confirmed booking.
3. Use **Accept and schedule** to choose the final schedule and confirm it, or propose another time/decline through the offered actions. Check whether an alternative is awaiting the client; replace/withdraw it only through the available controls.
4. Verify the final schedule and linked job, including assignment. An active client relationship, available service location, and valid future schedule are prerequisites. Client-record corrections or worker assignment may need other delegated authority.

Availability information does not guarantee conflict prevention or cancel existing commitments. Ask workers/Owner about conflicts. Do not use the sales Requests pipeline as proof that a Job Request has been scheduled.

## 7. Inspections, Work Approval, and Rework

**Requires for inspection: visibility of the job and an open inspection cycle. Approval requires Manage Quality Review; corrections require Request rework.**

1. Open the permitted job and review its checklist, notes, available photos, and inspection history.
2. For an inspection, use the offered inspection action, enter a readiness score from 1–10, review severity/issues/notes, add supported photos if needed, and submit. Verify the saved inspection. A closed cycle must be reopened by the Owner.
3. On Submitted work, use the approval action only with quality-review authority, or **Request Rework** with clear correction notes if authorized. Verify Approved or Rework Requested.
4. Rework requires another execution/submission round before review. Do not confuse your inspection record with final approval or a cleaner's submission.

Inspection access is separate from the approval permission. Red-flag resolution is another permission. None of these authorizes compensation approval or payment. Use only the actions available for the job's current state.

## 8. Red Flags

**Viewing follows permitted job visibility. Changes require Resolve red flags.**

1. Open **Red Flags** or a permitted job's issues. Read description, category, and Low/Medium/High/Critical severity.
2. If authorized, use the offered lifecycle action and explanatory note to mark the issue in progress, resolved, or not being fixed as appropriate.
3. Verify the resulting status. Terminal issues cannot be changed through the normal Manager lifecycle controls. Escalate urgent safety/access problems directly to the Owner.

Normal form submission does not automatically report every problem. A red flag is not a replacement for required inventory or checklist work. Do not assume Owner-only maintenance-creation or general issue controls are available to you.

## 9. Workers, Teams, and Invitations

**Requires: Manage team operations.**

1. Open **Workers** for the company directory and supported team controls. Directory visibility is not unrestricted access to Owner Worker Detail.
2. Create/manage teams and active membership, identify leads, and verify the saved team. Assigning that team to work additionally needs assignment authority in the relevant workflow.
3. Invite ordinary Cleaners or Maintenance workers using the offered role/email controls. Check invitation status and use permitted resend/revoke actions when needed. An accepted invitation means the worker should sign in; it is not a reason to send duplicates.
4. Escalate privileged worker profile, pay, compliance, role, or account-status changes to the Owner. Worker-PDF management is separately delegated through document permission.

Managers cannot create other Managers, change Manager permissions, or manage privileged invitations as if they were the Owner. Team authority does not approve compensation.

## 10. Administering Cleaner Fast Mode

**Requires: Manage team operations.** This is administration for Cleaners, not Fast Mode access for your own Manager account.

1. In **Workers**, find an active Cleaner and change **Allow Fast Mode**. Verify the eligibility change saved.
2. Explain that each form still starts in Standard Mode. The eligible Cleaner intentionally activates Fast Mode, visually checks every task, then chooses **Confirm [Section] Complete** for each section.
3. Revoke eligibility through the same control when required. Future section confirmations are blocked, and updated forms return to Standard Mode without undoing previous completions/history.

Eligibility does not automatically mark work complete or bypass inventory, actual performed-worker confirmation, or final submission. Section confirmation records an explicit confirmation, not proof of physical cleaning quality. Maintenance and Manager execution roles do not qualify for Cleaner Fast Mode.

## 11. Clients, Portal Access, and Resource Sharing

**Requires: Manage clients and properties. Managing the resource library separately requires Manage documents.**

1. Open **Clients** to find/create the correct relationship. Review active status, contact details, and linked locations before duplicating a record.
2. Use the supported access/invitation controls with the correct email. Check the invitation/access result; creating a client record alone does not create portal credentials.
3. In the client record, explicitly assign an available resource through its sharing controls. Verify the assignment; upload alone does not make a file visible to a client.
4. Follow linked records only for workflows you are authorized to manage. Sales documents and financial records require their own permissions.

If you can manage sales but not client access, ask the authorized Owner/colleague to resolve missing portal access. Do not substitute an email address for a verified client relationship.

## 12. Leads, Walkthroughs, Proposals, and Agreements

**Requires: Manage sales and commercial operations. Client access, job creation, assignment, schedules, documents, and invoices have separate permissions.**

1. Open **Requests** or its pipeline. Review public/manual leads, contact/location details, notes, and next follow-up.
2. Where needed, use **Walkthrough** to schedule, record findings, and complete an assessment. A proposal created from that walkthrough requires completion first. Manager calendars do not currently show these appointments; check the Request itself.
3. Create/edit the proposal, review scope, frequency, price, add-ons, and recipient, then save before review/send. Verify delivery and accepted/declined state. Use the provided return-to-draft controls before changing sent material.
4. From an accepted proposal, create an agreement when appropriate. Save/review, use offered ready/send and client-response/outside-signing actions, and verify document state and issue history.
5. Separately convert/schedule work only with the required authority and linked records. Confirm the resulting job/account; proposal acceptance or an agreement alone does not schedule a job.

A pending delivery, finalized document, missing email/access, or inactive client can block progress. Ask the authorized person to complete the missing responsibility rather than bypassing it.

## 13. Commercial Accounts and Schedules

**Requires for accounts: Manage sales and commercial operations. Recurrence administration additionally requires Manage schedules and Job Requests; assignment work needs its own authority.**

1. Open **Commercial Accounts** and verify client/location links, account status, source proposal/agreement, and permitted assignments.
2. If the account offers **Schedule** and the controls load for your delegated duties, review recurrence, dates, service details, and authorized assignment before saving.
3. Explicitly generate jobs for an allowed range, up to **90 days per generation**, with an active account and schedule. Verify generated jobs rather than assuming a saved recurrence populated future work.
4. Inspect existing jobs separately when service is paused, resumed, or ended. Use commercial billing only with invoice authority.

Some permission combinations do not provide a usable schedule screen. If controls fail or are absent, stop and ask the Owner to handle scheduling and review your intended responsibilities. Do not request unrelated permissions simply to make a screen load. Sales permission alone does not grant scheduling, assignment, or invoicing.

## 14. Documents and Worker PDFs

**Requires: Manage documents for Resources, Team Documents, Templates, and worker PDFs; Manage sales and commercial operations for Client Documents. Client resource sharing uses client-management authority.**

1. Open **Documents** in Company navigation. Only your authorized sections appear. **Client Documents** lists proposals/agreements and their issue history; continue edits/actions from their original Request or Commercial Account.
2. In **Resources**, manage permitted company files. In **Templates**, save/review supported agreement templates and defaults for new agreements. Changes do not rewrite every existing issued document.
3. Open **Worker Onboarding Documents**, or the Team Documents management link, to upload/manage supported worker PDFs, required status, audience, and visibility. Verify that the correct PDF is uploaded and active.
4. For client visibility, use the explicit assignment workflow in section 11. Document-library administration alone is not client-access authority.

Worker acknowledgments/reviews do not establish that a signed PDF is stored. **Manuals** App Guides explain SCRUB; company cleaning procedures and uploaded manuals are different resources. This guide is available even without document-management permission.

## 15. Invoices Versus Read-Only Financial Reporting

**Requires: Manage invoices for invoice changes. View financials grants reporting/read-only access, not outgoing-payment authority.**

1. If authorized to manage invoices, open **Invoices** or the supported billing panel on permitted work. Check approved work, accepted pricing, active client links, and whether it was already invoiced.
2. Review/create a draft, save, issue, and send when recipient/access prerequisites are ready. Verify status and payment evidence; sending does not mean paid. Use only offered invoice actions, with paid/issued/draft restrictions.
3. With financial-view authority, use **Financials** and **Payments** to inspect supported invoice/compensation balances and history. Invoice details reached through supported links may be read-only; financial viewing does not always provide a separate Invoices navigation item.
4. Ask the Owner to approve compensation, correct Owner-only obligations, or record outgoing worker/partner payments. Do not treat work approval or a reported balance as permission to send or record money.

Invoice management does not grant unrestricted financial reporting. Client invoice payment records are separate from outgoing compensation. Legacy settlement history does not authorize new settlement Checkout or establish current debt. Company Stripe setup remains Owner-only.

## 16. Analytics, Operational Settings, and Owner-Only Boundaries

**Requires: View analytics for Performance/Analytics; Manage Operational Settings for supported Add-Ons and Inventory Templates.**

1. Open the permitted reporting page and check its measures/date range. Self-scores, inspections, red flags, and operational completion measures are not interchangeable or complete financial accounts.
2. Open authorized **Add-Ons** or **Inventory Templates**, make the supported change, save, and verify it. Do not assume it rewrites existing job/document snapshots.
3. Escalate Owner responsibilities: Manager invitations/permissions, company identity/subscription/Stripe setup, privileged worker profile/pay/compliance controls, compensation approval/outgoing payments, and inspection-cycle reopening.

Operational settings do not open the Owner Settings root. Owner site/partner administration and platform administration are separate; Manager status grants neither. Use only explicitly available account functions, not another role's guide as evidence of authority.

## 17. Troubleshooting and Escalation

- **Missing page/action:** confirm enabled responsibilities, company access, and record state with the Owner. Permissions are independent and may change while you are signed in.
- **Can view but cannot execute:** verify direct worker assignment or active team membership. Oversight assignment and all-jobs visibility are insufficient; Maintenance execution and Cleaner Fast Mode are not Manager capabilities.
- **New job not in My Jobs:** check oversight/executor/team assignment or ask an authorized colleague to locate it. Do not create another job to recover visibility.
- **Closed inspection or unavailable quality action:** check the inspection cycle, submitted state, and your approval/rework/issue authority. Only the Owner reopens the cycle.
- **Permission changed or page fails:** stop the action, reopen the record or refresh, and confirm what saved. If access remains unavailable, ask the Owner; do not repeatedly retry or request unrelated rights as a workaround.
- **Payment/document discrepancy:** identify invoice versus compensation versus legacy history, review saved status/evidence and recipient/access, then involve the authorized person before duplicating an action.

Give the Owner the job/client/date, intended action, and displayed error. Never share passwords or invitation links. Use the [Cleaner App Guide](/manuals/cleaner) for authorized execution details, or contact SCRUB support at scrubadubsolutionsllc@gmail.com for an unresolved application problem.
